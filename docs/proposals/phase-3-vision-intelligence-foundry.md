## Executive Summary

The current AI market has converged around a dominant user interface paradigm: the conversational assistant.

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others have successfully introduced billions of people to AI through a simple interaction:

"Ask a question, receive intelligence."

However, this interface represents only the first layer of the AI revolution.

The next major opportunity is not simply accessing intelligence, but creating, owning, deploying, and monetizing specialized intelligence.

This proposal recommends that Aigarth Cloud evolve beyond being a platform where users run artificial neural networks (ANNs) into becoming:

**The Intelligence Foundry**

A decentralized ecosystem where individuals, organizations, researchers, and governments can create, train, deploy, own, and exchange specialized artificial intelligence.

Rather than competing directly with general-purpose AI assistants, Aigarth Cloud can establish a new category:

The operating system and marketplace for user-created intelligence.

## Proposed Phase: Intelligence Foundry

### Vision

Transform Aigarth Cloud into a platform where users can visually create AI entities from:

- Data
- Goals
- Training parameters
- Feedback loops
- Compute resources

The result: a deployed ANN that can be owned, improved, shared, or monetized.

## Why This Creates Strategic Value

### 1. Creates a New Category

- **Current AI:** "Access intelligence."
- **Aigarth:** "Create intelligence."

This moves Aigarth from being a technology platform into an economic ecosystem.

### 2. Creates Network Effects

Every new ANN increases the value of the ecosystem.

More users create:

- More intelligence assets
- More datasets
- More applications
- More marketplace activity
- More compute demand

The platform compounds.

### 3. Gives Qubic Utility Beyond Computation

Qubic becomes the economic layer for:

- Training
- Hosting
- Access
- Licensing
- Intelligence transactions

**Example flow:**

```
Developer creates ANN
        ↓
ANN deployed on Aigarth
        ↓
Users consume intelligence
        ↓
Revenue distributed through Qubic economy
```

### 4. Enables AI Sovereignty

Governments, companies, universities, and individuals increasingly require:

- Private AI
- Data ownership
- Custom intelligence
- Transparent systems

Aigarth can provide:

> "Your intelligence, your data, your ownership."
