## 1. Mission

Turn Qubic's technical capabilities into a globally accessible AI infrastructure ecosystem, so QUBIC gains real utility from the products built on top of it.

## 2. Business Model: Seven Revenue Streams

Aigarth Cloud generates revenue from seven streams. The composition shifts over the program as the marketplace flywheel matures (more ANNs leads to more usage leads to more marketplace activity leads to more staker yield leads to more staking leads to more capacity leads to more usage).

| # | Stream | Pricing model | Year 1 target share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI API subscriptions | Per-token / per-call metering, in QUBIC | 70% |
| 2 | Enterprise plans | Annual contracts, custom | 15% |
| 3 | ANN marketplace fees | 10% of license / subscription revenue | 8% |
| 4 | Compute marketplace fees | 5–15% of transaction value | 4% |
| 5 | Managed AI infrastructure | Monthly retainer | 2% |
| 6 | Hardware ecosystem sales | One-time + subscription | 1% |
| 7 | Developer services | Training, certification, support | < 1% |

The unit economics are illustrative today; the actuals will land once production is live and will be published in the quarterly update starting at the end of Milestone 1.

## 3. Capital Formation

- **Total ask:** 100,000,000,000 QUBIC (one hundred billion).
- **Structure:** four milestone-gated tranches of 25,000,000,000 QUBIC each.
- **Disbursement:** USD-indexed at release, against the QUBIC market price on the release date. We do not assume a particular price.
- **First payment:** end of month 3, on QA sign-off of Milestone 1.
- **Last payment:** end of month 12, on QA sign-off of Milestone 4.

**Working capital for months 0–3** is covered from non-tranche sources (founder capital, with a working-capital facility as backstop). The full plan is in [`annex-cash-flow.md`](./annex-cash-flow.md). The summary: we can deliver Milestone 1 without drawing on Tranche 1.

**What we are not asking for:** a token sale, an upfront payment, a bridge loan denominated in QUBIC, or any structure that would dilute existing QUBIC holders. The ask is a milestone-gated grant, paid in QUBIC, USD-indexed at release.

## 4. Market and Competition

| Competitor category | What we learn from them | Where we differ |
|---|---|---|
| Hyperscalers (AWS, GCP, Azure) | Predictable economics, OpenAI compatibility | We do not compete on hyperscaler breadth. We compete on Qubic integration and on being purpose-built for AI. |
| Foundation model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) | Pricing, UX, developer experience | We do not compete on frontier model capability. We compete on openness, cost, and ANN ownership. |
| Model hubs (Hugging Face) | Marketplace mechanics, creator tooling | We do not compete on model-hub breadth in Year 1. We compete on integrated compute and on the Qubic settlement layer. |
| AI compute clouds (Together, Anyscale) | OpenAI compatibility, GPU economics | We do not compete on GPU-first positioning. We compete on Qubic's UPoW as the underlying compute substrate. |
| Decentralised compute (Render, Akash, io.net) | Marketplace UX, node operator onboarding | We do not compete on general compute. We compete on AI-specific tooling and on Qubic integration. |

The wedge is Qubic. The competitor that tries to replicate Aigarth Cloud has to ship on Qubic, integrate with the aigarthpool, and win the Qubic community. That is not a near-term threat.

## 5. Sustainability and Long-Term Plan

Tranche 4 funds Milestone 4. It does not fund months 13–24. From month 13 onward, the platform is funded by:

1. Operating cash from the seven revenue streams above, growing month-over-month.
2. Strategic commercial partnerships: cloud marketplace listings, system integrators, vertical partners.
3. A subsequent funding round, only if 1 and 2 are insufficient.
4. A founder follow-on, only with explicit Qubic alignment.

A twelve-month minimum commitment, a twenty-four-month public roadmap, and the post-program funding ladder are detailed in [`06-commitment.md`](./06-commitment.md) and [`annex-commitment.md`](./annex-commitment.md). The short version: the program accelerates the platform into commercial operation. The business sustains itself.

## 6. Return Mechanism to Qubic

Aigarth Cloud will establish ecosystem value-sharing mechanisms during the program. The structure will be finalised with the Incubation Board and the Qubic community during the Adaptation phase, but the intended mechanisms are:

- **Revenue contribution to ecosystem development** — a defined share of platform revenue directed to the Incubation Fund or to a Qubic-controlled development pool.
- **Developer grant support** — sponsoring ecosystem grants for ANNs, tooling, and integrations that use Aigarth Cloud.
- **Infrastructure expansion** — funding for additional Qubic node operators and ANN training capacity that strengthens the network.
- **Community initiatives** — funding for the Aigarth Observatory and other community-facing surfaces that bring new users into the Qubic ecosystem.

The exact percentages, schedule, and counterparty will be agreed in writing with the Incubation Team during the Adaptation phase. The default position is: Aigarth Cloud shares back a portion of revenue proportional to the share of capacity that comes from Qubic-ecosystem node operators, audited annually.
