# Aigarth Cloud: Building the Commercial AI Infrastructure Layer for Qubic

> Submission to the [Qubic Incubation Program](https://qubic.org/incubation-program).
> Total ask: 100,000,000,000 QUBIC, four milestone-gated tranches of 25B each, USD-indexed at release.
> Program horizon: 12 months.
> License: Apache 2.0.

---

## Executive Summary

Aigarth Cloud is the commercial AI infrastructure layer for Qubic: an OpenAI-compatible API gateway, an Artificial Neural Network (ANN) marketplace, and a decentralized compute marketplace that turn Qubic's Useful Proof of Work and ANN primitives into products developers and enterprises will pay for.

The platform converts QUBIC from a transfer token into productive infrastructure. Users stake QUBIC to receive compute allocation. ANN creators publish, price, and monetise their models in QUBIC. Node operators contribute capacity and earn from network usage. The result: a closed-loop economy where the token gains utility every time a developer ships an application.

We are not a model lab, not a hyperscaler, not a wrapper on someone else's API. We are the access layer that makes Qubic's capabilities commercially consumable.

By the end of the twelve-month program, Aigarth Cloud will have a public alpha with 1,000 active developers, 50 published ANNs, 1,000,000 compute hours, and 10 enterprise pilots, all running on Qubic mainnet.

## Funding ask at a glance

- **Total ask:** 100,000,000,000 QUBIC.
- **Structure:** four milestone-gated tranches of 25B QUBIC each.
- **Disbursement:** USD-indexed at release, against the QUBIC market price on the release date.
- **First payment:** end of month 3, on QA sign-off of Milestone 1.
- **Last payment:** end of month 12, on QA sign-off of Milestone 4.

Full detail in [`01-business-plan.md`](./01-business-plan.md). Cash-flow plan for the unfunded months 0–3 in [`annex-cash-flow.md`](./annex-cash-flow.md).

## Closing

Qubic has the technical primitives. What is missing is the access layer that turns those primitives into products developers and enterprises will pay for. Aigarth Cloud is that access layer.

The team has shipped 23+ delivery phases, designed the platform end-to-end, and structured the program into four milestones with objective, user-perspective acceptance criteria. The funding ask is milestone-gated, USD-indexed, and sized to the work. The compliance posture, the cash-flow plan, and the twenty-four-month roadmap are documented.

By the end of month 12, Aigarth Cloud will be a production AI infrastructure layer on Qubic, with active developers, paying enterprises, and a self-sustaining revenue model. The program's success is not the end of the work. It is the start of the business.
